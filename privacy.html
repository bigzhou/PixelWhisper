1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Shanghai Pixel Whisper Online Game Co., Ltd. ("Pixel Whisper", "we") publishes WhisperGames2026, also referred to as whisperminigames2026 (Apple bundle identifier: com.whispergames.minigames2026). This policy covers the current offline Apple-platform version of that app and this company website. It does not automatically describe other games or third-party platforms with different features.

2. Information in the game

The current app does not require an account, track players across apps or websites, or upload personal information or gameplay data to our servers. It does not include advertising or third-party analytics services.

Game progress, scores, unlocks and preferences are stored locally on your device so the game can resume and remember your choices. Local file metadata, preferences and timing information are used for app functionality. We do not receive this local data unless you choose to send it to us for support.

3. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to provide. We use these to respond, troubleshoot and handle your request. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

4. Apple services and this website

App Store downloads, operating-system backups and optional platform diagnostics are handled by Apple under its own settings and privacy policy. These services are separate from our offline game. We do not operate a game cloud-save service.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser information and access logs to provide and secure hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Our website does not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts or registration forms. Following an external link or emailing us involves that service's own privacy practices.

5. Sharing, storage and security

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising. Support correspondence is handled through our email service provider and may be disclosed when legally required. Email and hosting providers may process information outside your country under their own applicable policies.

We keep support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and applicable legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards for information we receive, but cannot guarantee absolute security. Local saves remain under your device's app storage and backup settings.

6. Your choices and requests

You can reset game progress using the app's available save controls. Removing the app and managing device backups are handled by your operating system; backups or older app preferences may require separate removal. We cannot remotely access or delete a save that exists only on your device.

For information you sent to us, contact us to request access, correction or deletion, or to raise a privacy concern. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law. Depending on your location, additional rights, including a complaint to a data protection authority, may apply.

7. Children

Offline play does not require a child to provide personal information. We do not knowingly request children's personal information through the game. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information can contact us to request its removal.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated as required. A future version with online services, advertising or analytics will need an updated policy and relevant platform disclosures before those practices begin.

Shanghai Pixel Whisper Online Game Co., Ltd.（品牌名称 Pixel Whisper，以下简称“我们”）发布 WhisperGames2026，也称 whisperminigames2026（Apple 应用标识：com.whispergames.minigames2026）。本政策适用于该应用当前的 Apple 平台离线版本，以及本公司官网；不自动适用于功能不同的其他游戏或第三方平台版本。

当前应用无需注册账号，不跨应用或网站跟踪玩家，不向我们的服务器上传个人信息或游戏数据，也未接入广告或第三方统计分析服务。

游戏进度、分数、解锁内容及偏好设置保存在您的设备本地，用于恢复进度、记住选择。本地文件元数据、偏好和计时信息用于实现应用功能。除非您主动向我们发送相关内容以获取支持，我们不会收到这些本地数据。

如果您发送邮件，我们会收到您的邮箱地址，以及您主动提供的内容和附件。这些信息用于回复、排查问题及处理请求。请勿发送密码、支付资料或其他不必要的敏感信息。

5. 共享、保存与安全

我们不出售个人信息，不为跨应用跟踪或定向广告共享个人信息。支持邮件通过邮件服务商处理，也可能依法向有权机关提供。邮件及托管服务商可能按其适用政策在您所在国家或地区之外处理信息。

离线游戏不要求儿童提供个人信息，我们不会通过游戏有意索取儿童个人信息。如果监护人认为儿童曾向我们发送个人信息，可以联系我们申请删除。

信息处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页面及上述日期，并按要求告知重大变更。未来版本如增加在线服务、广告或统计功能，应在实施相应处理前更新本政策及平台隐私披露。
